# Murmur 1.0.0

Free, offline voice dictation for Windows. Hold **Ctrl + Alt**, speak, let go, and your words appear wherever you can type. No account, no subscription, no word limits, and your voice never leaves your computer.

## Install

1. Download **Murmur.zip** below.
2. Right-click it → **Extract All…** and put the folder somewhere permanent (e.g. `Documents\Murmur`).
3. Double-click **install.bat** and wait 5 to 15 minutes.

The installer installs Python if needed, detects whether you have an NVIDIA graphics card, downloads the right speech model, and adds Murmur to your desktop and Start Menu.

> If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 8 GB RAM, 2 to 5 GB free disk space
- **With an NVIDIA GPU (4 GB+):** Whisper Turbo on the GPU, under a second per sentence
- **Without one (AMD/Intel graphics or none):** set up automatically on the processor with the Small model, 1 to 3 seconds per sentence

Full details and troubleshooting: [README](https://github.com/Abhijay-Nagal/murmur#readme)

## What's inside

- Push-to-talk and hands-free dictation in any app
- Whisper large-v3-turbo on your GPU, with automatic CPU fallback
- Optional local AI cleanup through Ollama (removes fillers, handles self-corrections, formats lists)
- Custom vocabulary, spoken shortcuts and voice commands ("new line")
- Searchable history and stats (words per minute, time saved, streak)
- Floating listening indicator, tray icon, start with Windows
